### Research Theme
---
<p align="justify">
Sometimes, even the smartest software engineers and SOTA compilers
<a href="https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-172-performance-engineering-of-software-systems-fall-2018/resources/mit6_172f18_lec9/">fall short</a>  of performance demands. 
When every <a href="https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-172-performance-engineering-of-software-systems-fall-2018/resources/mit6_172f18_lec1/">software trick</a> has been exhausted, 
and we still need more; Who brings performance beyond the reach of code? <a href ="" style="font-size: 1rem; color: #FF7F50;">Coops</a> and I certainly do. 
We extract raw performance from the veins of silicon itself. We tap into performance reservoirs that no software has access to. We unlock performance pathways which are abstracted away from even compilers. 
We reclaim performance at every
<a href="https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/docs/programmable/784383/24-1/cpu-cycles-and-steps.html">CPU tick</a>. Welcome to the world of
<a href="https://developer.arm.com/documentation/102404/0202/Architecture-and-micro-architecture" style="font-size: 1rem; color: #1E90FF; font-weight: normal;">Microarchitects</a>,
