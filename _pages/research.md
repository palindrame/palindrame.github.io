### Research Theme
---

When even the best software devs hit performance bottlenecks and [compilers](https://cs.brown.edu/courses/csci1260/spring-2021/lectures/what-is-a-compiler.html)
run out of [clever tricks](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/6-172-performance-engineering-of-software-systems-fall-2018/resources/mit6_172f18_lec9/), 
<a href="https://developer.arm.com/documentation/102404/0202/Architecture-and-micro-architecture" 
    style="font-size: 1rem; color: #1E90FF; font-weight: normal;">
    microarchitects
</a>
step in to save the day. We tap into performance reservoirs, no software has access to. We unlock performance pathways, abstracted away from developers; and even the compiler.
We operate at nanosecond levels of granularity, and extract every last bit of performance gain, measured in CPU
[clock cycles](https://www.intel.com/content/www/us/en/docs/programmable/784383/24-1/cpu-cycles-and-steps.html).


But it's not just performance we care about
